HOW THE INDEX WORKS

From airfare observations to a national indicator.

Individual airfare observations are normalized and stored in the database. Route × booking-window prices are aggregated using a Jevons geometric mean, followed by weighted Laspeyres and Paasche calculations. Their geometric mean forms the Fisher Ideal Index.

Observations → Jevons → Price Relatives → Laspeyres → Paasche → Fisher
